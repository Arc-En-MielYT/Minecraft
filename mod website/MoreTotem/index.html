More Totem

More Totem is a Minecraft mod which adds more totem in the game !

Some are (very) easy to get, but some are (very) harder to get, can you craft them all ?

Need some help with the crafts and effects ? Here you go !

^^Coal Totem Craft : 5 of any type of dirt. No effect, respawn with 0.5pv.

^^Iron Totem Craft : 1 iron block, 4 iron ingots. No effect, respawn with 1.5pv and a full iron stuff.

^^Gold Totem Craft : 1 gold bloc, 4 gold ingots. Absorption I for 5s, respawn with 3.5pv, full gold stuff, 16 golden carrot and 4 golden apple.

^^Diamond Totem Craft : 2 diamond bloc, 3 diamond. Absorption I for 30s, Regeneration I for 15s, respawn with 5pv and a full diamond stuff.

^^Netherite Totem Craft : 2 netherite bloc, 3 netherite scrap. Absorption II for 45s, Regeneration III for 20s, Fire Resistence I for 20s, respawn with 7.5pv and a full netherite stuff. You can also upgrade your Diamond Totem with 8 netherite scrap. Don't burn in fire or lava.

^^Ultimate Totem Craft : 3 Diamond Totem, 4 Gold Totem, 2 Netherite Totem. Absorption V, Regeneration III, Fire Resistance III, Resistance V, Saturation V, Water Breathing V, Haste III, Strength III, Speed II, Night Vision II, Slow Falling II for 3 years and 4 month (max ticks java accept). Don't burn in fire or lava.

After using the Ultimate Totem, you will get the Used Ultimate Totem. DON'T TRY TO DIE WITH IT ! You can use it for decoration as it's the hardest to get ! Don't burn in fire or lava.
